Overview

A top-tier data center in the central U.S.

Exelare is hosted in a 20,000 sq ft data center in St. Louis, rated top tier for network connectivity and infrastructure design. It occupies an 18-floor, all-steel-frame secure building that is one of the largest telecom facilities in the central U.S.

Five levels of physical security.

Level 1

Locked to the public

Customers submit a photo and ID verification to receive an access card. Every entry is logged.

Level 2

Guards on duty

Security guards are on site 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Level 3

Biometric scanner

A biometric hand scanner is required for all employees and customers.

Level 4

Steel doors and badges

Steel doors and proximity badge locks protect server areas. Entries are logged by name, date and time.

Level 5

Locked cabinets

Cabinets are locked, with keys or access codes managed by hosting staff.

Monitoring

Always watched

The facility is monitored 24/7 by recorded security cameras and on-site staff.

The infrastructure behind it.

Network

Direct multi-gigabit connections to four tier 1 international backbone providers, each entering through a different route: Verizon/UUNET, CenturyLink/Qwest, Level 3 and Cogent.

BGP routing and redundant 10Gb/GigE fiber on diverse physical paths. Traffic avoids public exchange points.

Power

Two underground grids in a spot network, so either grid can drop without interrupting building power.

Onsite diesel generators with priority refueling contracts and four UPS systems provide backup, and the infrastructure is tested regularly.

Cooling

Redundant computer room AC units control temperature and humidity year-round. Each has independent compressors and cooling loops and runs on normal and backup generator power.

Proactive temperature monitoring, redundant water tanks, and dual water mains with diverse entries.

Fire suppression

Concrete floors, steel ceilings, fireproof walls and steel-framed racks, with no flammable materials inside the data center.

VESDA air sampling detects early combustion by-products. A zoned, dry-pipe, pre-action sprinkler system needs two or more sensors to activate, which prevents accidental discharge.
