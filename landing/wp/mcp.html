What MCP is, in plain words.

MCP stands for Model Context Protocol, the open standard for connecting AI agents to software. Exelare MCP is that connection for your ATS.

The chat

Your AI agent

The AI assistant your team already uses. It needs to support custom connectors.

The connection

Exelare MCP

The connector between the two. You add it once, sign in with your usual Exelare username and password, and allow access.

The source

Your Exelare data

Candidates, jobs, contacts, activities, AI Matching and reports. The agent only sees what your Exelare account is allowed to see.

How it works

Connect once. Then just ask.

  1. 01
    Add Exelare as a connectorIn your AI agent's connector settings, add a custom connector, name it Exelare and paste the Exelare MCP address.
  2. 02
    Sign in and allow accessUse your usual Exelare username and password, read what the agent is asking to do, then allow it.
  3. 03
    Ask in plain wordsStart a chat with the Exelare connector turned on. You only connect once.
